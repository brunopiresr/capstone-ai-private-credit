## Interpretation

Labels were generated by text heuristics in `run_and_label.py`, not independent
human verification. The 70% rate describes those labels only. It is not measured
financial accuracy, and these cases are separate from the 60-scenario diagnostic suite.

Happy paths received 13/14 heuristic passes, while out-of-scope cases received only
2/8 and breaking cases 3/8. These results do not support claims of consistent scope
redirection or broadly reliable guardrails. Inspect actual answers and tool evidence
before treating an individual heuristic pass as correct.
